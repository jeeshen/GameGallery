# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Friends revisiting screenshots from games they played together.

## Product Purpose
A personal photo gallery with a separate album for every game. Browse albums, see their photos, and open individual memories at full size.

## Brand Personality
Personal, cinematic, bold. The user selected Orkan as the visual reference: vivid orange, oversized typography, and immersive photography.

## Anti-references
Game storefronts, review platforms, and dashboards that distract from the photos.

## Design Principles
- Screenshots are the main attraction.
- Keep each game's memories together.
- Keep browsing and returning to albums simple.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
